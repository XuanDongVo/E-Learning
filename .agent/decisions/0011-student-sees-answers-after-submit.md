# 0011 — Student sees score and correct answers right after submitting
Status: Superseded by 0019
Date: 2026-10-07

## Historical decision

After an Assignment submission, the student could see score and correct answers immediately and there was no Release Answers step.

## Superseded rule

ADR 0019 keeps the immediate-review capability but makes answer visibility a teacher-controlled boolean:
show_answers_after_submit.

This historical ADR remains only as traceability. Active specs must follow ADR 0019.
